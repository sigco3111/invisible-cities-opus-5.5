Contents

Nine chapters. Eleven kinds of city, each told five times, braided in a descending pattern: every chapter opens a new thread and closes an old one. Click a city to travel there; click a chapter to read the conversation that follows it.

Each diorama is generated procedurally in the browser from a few hundred lines of code. The texts are paraphrases written for this atlas; read the book itself (trans. William Weaver, 1974).
